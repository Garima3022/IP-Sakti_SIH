About the Platform

What is DR. Ayurvedic Sathi?

An end-to-end, multilingual RAG-driven AI platform built for the Ministry of Ayush under the SIH IP-SAKTI Sahayak problem statement. It streamlines Ayurvedic drug compliance, intellectual property risk assessment, and global export regulatory pathways.

AI Regulatory Assistant

Get instant compliance guidance on CITES, Patent Act §3(p), FDA, EMA regulations. Powered by Gemini AI with topic-restricted responses ensuring accurate, domain-specific answers.

CITES & Wildlife Screening

Screens formulation ingredients against CITES Appendices I & II and Indian Wildlife Protection Act 1972. Flags endangered species with RED/AMBER alerts and recommends Pratinidhi Dravya substitutes.

Patent Act §3(p) Risk Analyzer

Evaluates traditional knowledge aggregation vs. synergistic non-obviousness. Computes risk scores and provides actionable steps to establish empirical synergy (CI < 0.8).

Herb Camera Scanner

Point your camera at any herb to instantly identify it — get the botanical name, Sanskrit name, medicinal uses, Rasa-Veerya-Vipaka profile, and CITES regulatory status.

Global Export Roadmap

Auto-generates regulatory pathways for US FDA (DSHEA/NDA), EU EMA (THMPD 30-yr rule), and GCC herbal medicine registration with step-by-step compliance checklists.

Multilingual Support

Supports English, Hindi, Tamil & Sanskrit with entity normalization. Normalizes vernacular plant names into Latin botanical binomials for accurate regulatory cross-referencing.
